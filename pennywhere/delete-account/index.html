Delete your Pennywhere account

In the app (fastest): open Pennywhere → tap your profile picture (top right) → scroll down → Delete my account and data → type DELETE to confirm.

By email: write to sudhindraks.dev@gmail.com from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We'll do it within 30 days and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, settings) and sign-in.
  • Your personal expenses, monthly totals and encryption keys.
  • Your membership of the household. When the last member leaves, the household and all its expenses are deleted.
  • Your Help & feedback messages, on request.

What is kept

  • A one-way hash of your email address, so the free month of Premium can't be claimed again. It can't be turned back into your email.
  • Records Google Play keeps about purchases you made (under Google's own policy).

Expenses shared with your household stay readable by the household's other members until they delete them or their account. See the privacy policy.

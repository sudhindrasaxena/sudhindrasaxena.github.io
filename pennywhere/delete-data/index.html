Delete some of your Pennywhere data (keep your account)

You can delete any of your data in the Pennywhere app without deleting your account.

Steps

  • One expense: open the Expenses tab → swipe the expense to the left. You have 5 seconds to tap Cancel; after that it is deleted from our servers.
  • Several expenses: in the Expenses tab, long-press an expense → tick the others → tap Delete.
  • An expense you're looking at: open it → Delete expense.
  • Your monthly budget: tap your profile picture (top right) → tap the Household or Personal budget → clear the amount → Remove budget.
  • Anything else — older monthly totals, grocery amounts, or your Help & feedback messages: email sudhindraks.dev@gmail.com from your account's email address and say what to delete. We'll do it within 30 days and confirm.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted data is removed from our servers straight away (or within 30 days for email requests). We keep no copies or backups of it.
  • Deleting a household expense removes it for every member of the household.
  • Your account, name, email, settings and Premium stay, so you can keep using the app.
  • Google Play keeps its own records of purchases you made, under Google's policy.

To delete everything instead, see delete your account. More in the privacy policy.

Pennywhere — Privacy policy

App: Pennywhere: Expense Tracker (Android, package com.sudhindra.expenses) · Developer: Sudhindra Saxena · Effective 16 October 2026

Pennywhere helps you and your household see where your money goes. It is built so that we can't read your expenses: they are encrypted on your phone before they are stored. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Receipts and bills you scan or upload are read on your phone. The photos and PDFs are not uploaded.
  • Voice input uses your phone's own speech recognition. The app does not record or keep audio. (If your phone uses Google's speech service, Google's privacy policy applies to that.)
  • Your encryption keys are kept in your phone's secure key store. Your recovery code is shown to you once; we keep it only on your phone so you can see it again after unlocking with your phone's lock.
  • App lock uses your phone's fingerprint/face/PIN; we never receive biometric data.

3. What we don't do

  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone for their own purposes.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, photos library, SMS or other apps.

4. Service providers

The app runs on Google services that process data only to provide the service to us:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Cloud Firestore database and Cloud Functions, hosted in Mumbai, India (asia-south1). Firebase privacy
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google Play Billing for purchases. Google privacy policy
  • ML Kit (Google) reads receipts on your phone; images are not sent to Google by the app.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Your expense data is additionally end-to-end encrypted.

5. Sharing inside your household

Household expenses are shared with the people you invite (with a one-time code that you approve). Personal expenses are visible only to you. Anyone you approve into your household can read the household's expenses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Individual expenses: this month and the previous month; after that, only monthly totals (and grocery amounts), kept for up to 24 months — all still end-to-end encrypted.
  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Help & feedback messages: until resolved, then deleted on request.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for your Premium and for Google Play's records.
  • The one-way email hash for the free trial is kept after account deletion, to prevent repeat trials.

7. Deleting your data

In the app: profile → Delete my account and data. This deletes your profile, your personal expenses and your keys; you leave your household, and the household and its data are deleted when its last member leaves. You can also email us to ask for deletion. See how to delete your account.

8. Children

Pennywhere is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

9. Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, export your recovery backup, and delete your account at any time. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you can ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, or raise a grievance, by emailing us.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above; important changes will also be shown in the app.

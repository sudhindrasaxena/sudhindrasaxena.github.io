Privacy Policy

QR Reader for Android · Effective 27 September 2026

In short
  • Your scans stay on your phone. What you scan and your scan history are never uploaded — not to us, not to anyone.
  • The camera and photos you pick are read on your device only and never stored or sent.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers — you can control personalised ads.
  • If you send feedback, we receive only what you type plus your app version and phone model.
  • Optional tips are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

QR Reader is developed and published by Sudhindra Saxena, an individual developer in India ("we", "us"). Contact: sudhindraks.dev@gmail.com.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not collect your name, contacts, location, or anything else from your phone. When you choose an action on a result — open a link, call, send an SMS or email, add a contact, join a Wi-Fi network, open maps or pay with a UPI app — the app hands that information to the app you choose; what happens there is covered by that app's privacy policy. Links are never opened without your tap.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect and use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be inferred), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. On supported Android versions, the ads SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving advertising APIs (Topics and attribution). Ads never receive your scans or history.

Your choices: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and an "Ad privacy choices" option lets you change it later. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by phone).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Tips (Google Play)

Tips are optional one-time purchases made through Google Play and unlock nothing. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we receive only a purchase confirmation (such as an order ID), never your card, UPI or bank details.

5. Service providers

We rely on these Google services, each governed by the Google Privacy Policy:

  • Google ML Kit (on-device code reading). It may contact Google to download updates and send anonymous performance and usage metrics about its own functioning — not images or scanned content.
  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (ads and consent).
  • Google Forms / Google Sheets (receiving feedback you choose to send).
  • Google Play Billing (tips).

6. Security and retention

Scan history on your phone is encrypted (AES-256). Feedback is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and kept in a private spreadsheet only we can access, for as long as needed to handle it (and at most 24 months). Scan history stays on your phone until you delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete feedback you sent, and to withdraw consent, by emailing sudhindraks.dev@gmail.com — this includes your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where applicable, the GDPR. Please mention roughly when you sent the feedback so we can find it. We'll reply within 30 days. Scan data is never with us, so you control it fully on your phone.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has sent us feedback, contact us and we will delete it.

Privacy Policy

GPS Camera for Android · Effective 21 October 2026

In short
  • Your photos and your location stay on your phone. The app has no account and no servers. Nothing is sent to us.
  • To put the street address on the stamp, the photo's coordinates are sent to Google's address service. You can turn this off in the Stamp tab; the stamp then shows the coordinates only.
  • Your location is used only while the app is open, to stamp it onto the photos you take and write it into the photo files.
  • Anyone you share a photo with can see where and when it was taken. That's the purpose of the stamp, so think before sharing.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers — you can control personalised ads. Premium (one payment through Google Play) removes the ads and the watermark.
  • No analytics and no tracking of our own.

3. How the phone finds your location and address

The app asks Android for your location through Google Play services (the "fused location provider"), or through the phone's own GPS if Play services aren't available. Your phone may use Wi-Fi and mobile network signals as well as GPS. If Google Location Accuracy is turned on in your phone's settings, Google processes that information under the Google Privacy Policy. This is part of Android, not something the app does, and you can turn it off in your phone's location settings.

The street address comes from Android's geocoder, which asks Google's address service through Google Play services. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. Only the coordinates are sent, never the photo. With Street address turned off, or without internet, nothing is sent.

4. Sharing photos

Each photo and video shows its place and time on the stamp, and also carries the location in its file data (EXIF), so that maps and gallery apps can place it. Kept originals carry the location too. Some apps and websites remove this file data when you upload; many don't. Share a photo only with people who may know where it was taken.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, in the Gallery and Settings — never over the camera. To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect and use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be inferred), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. On supported Android versions, the ads SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving advertising APIs (Topics and attribution). Ads never receive your photos, videos, their location or the GPS position the app reads.

Your choices: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and an "Ad privacy choices" option in Settings lets you change it later. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by phone). With Premium, no ads are shown at all.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Premium and tips (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time purchase that removes the "Taken with GPS Camera" watermark and the ads. Tips are optional and unlock nothing. Both are processed by Google Play under its own terms and privacy policy; we receive only a purchase confirmation (such as an order ID), never your card, UPI or bank details. The app remembers on your phone whether you have Premium, so it works offline; Google Play restores it on a new phone with the same Google account.

7. Security and retention

Nothing is stored with us: the app has no servers. Advertising and purchase data are held by Google as described above. Addresses are kept in the app's memory only while it runs. Photos and settings stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling doesn't delete photos you've already taken, because they're saved in your own Pictures folder.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your phone, and your ad choices as described in section 5. For any privacy question, including your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where it applies, the GDPR, email sudhindraks.dev@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
